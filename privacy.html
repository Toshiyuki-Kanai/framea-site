Privacy Policy

Framea respects your privacy.

Information Collection

Framea does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Local File Access

Framea only accesses video files and folders that you explicitly choose. These files are processed locally on your Mac and are never uploaded to any server.

Metadata

If enabled, Framea may read metadata such as shooting date and location from video files in order to save it to exported images. This processing is performed locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

Framea does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking services.
